Two-tier support, quantified. “Silent ≥2 yr” counts boards whose listing has had no driver update of any kind in over two years — not slow support, none. MSI and ASUS maintain their entire catalogues; ASRock ships the most variants per chipset (~15 SKUs) and has left 65 boards — 18% of its catalogue — fully silent. Notably these are not its oldest boards: they are still-sold LGA1700 B760/Z790 models untouched since early 2024, while its AM4 boards keep receiving occasional chipset refreshes.

Use the data

Everything here is static JSON, CORS-enabled, versioned, and free to mirror. Point a driver-checker at by-hwid and report anything below the water line.

/v1/latest/water-level.jsonNewest known version per driver family
/v1/latest/vendor-lag.jsonThe vendor-lag metric above
/v1/latest/families.jsonFamily table with HWID sets
/v1/latest/boards.jsonBoard catalogue with chipset / socket
/v1/latest/by-hwid/{hwid}.jsonPoint lookup by hardware ID
⟠ GitHub repository 📄 Design spec 🔎 Findings { } Raw JSON

What “latest” means. The newest version any vendor has published, not a judgement that it is good — a vendor may legitimately withhold a driver that regressed. That caveat does not excuse a board sitting years behind on a leaking driver.

The water line is a floor. Since 2026-08-27 the index also tracks the Windows Update Catalog (per HWID) and silicon-vendor download pages (AMD, Intel) as upstream references: for 14 of 49 driver families the upstream already ships newer than any board vendor lists — AMD's own chipset driver is five point-releases ahead of every board vendor — so every lag number here is a lower bound. Upstream rows are labelled (upstream_only in water-level.json) and never counted against a vendor.

Caveats. Lag is measured against what vendors list, on Windows 11 x64 only. Cross-vendor version reconciliation is imperfect where vendors use incompatible numbering schemes for the same silicon (e.g. Realtek LAN), so a few individual family water-levels are approximate; the aggregate vendor metrics are robust to this. Method, sources, and every known wrinkle are in the findings doc.

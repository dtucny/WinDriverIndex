How current are each vendor's listings?

Ranked best to worst by median lag — the typical gap between a listed driver and the newest version any source ships for that silicon (board vendors plus Windows Update and AMD/Intel direct). The damning numbers: the share of a vendor's machines more than a year behind on at least one driver family, and the share whose listings have had no driver update at all in two years.

Median-lag track spans 0 → 2 years.

Full metric

Do vendors maintain their back catalogue?

Median worst-case lag per desktop platform, plus each vendor's add-in graphics cards (laptop/desktop system vendors have no socket and sit out this view). Darker = further behind: the tell-tale pattern is a vendor keeping the current generation fresh while old-but-still-sold boards drift for years.

Best & worst maintained, by vendor

Every vendor keeps some machines current and lets others rot — each vendor's best-kept and most-neglected per product type (a graphics card and a motherboard aren't the same race), broken down by component. The chips show where the lag is: one stale driver reads very differently from a machine abandoned across the board.

Use the data

Everything here is static JSON, CORS-enabled, versioned, and free to mirror. Point a driver-checker at by-hwid and report anything below the water line.
